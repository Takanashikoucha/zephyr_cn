MCUmgr

Management

以下

*
:ref:`OS

*
:ref:`Image

*
:ref:`Statistics

:ref:`Settings

*
:ref:`File

*
:ref:`Shell
management<mcumgr_smp_group_9>`
*
:ref:`Enumeration
management<mcumgr_smp_group_10>`

:ref:`Transport

:ref:`Zephyr

Management

Management

:zephyr_file:`subsys/mgmt/`

:zephyr:code-sample:`sample
<smp-svr>`

它

management

Bluetooth
LE
和
